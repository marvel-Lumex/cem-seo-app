Delete your Cem SEO account

You can delete your Cem SEO account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cem SEO and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email contact@lumexalliance.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Cem SEO account. We'll delete the account and confirm by reply, normally within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your name, email address, password hash, your tracked sites, keywords, audit history, notification settings, and any connected Google Search Console access tokens. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

What may be kept

Records we are legally required to keep (for example, a payment record held by our payment processor, Paystack) may be retained for the period the law requires. Error logs that don't identify you are removed on a short rolling schedule.
