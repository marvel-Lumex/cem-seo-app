Cem SEO

Smarter SEO. Stronger Rankings.

Cem SEO is a mobile app by Lumex Alliance that helps website owners understand and improve how their sites perform in Google Search — all from their phone.

What Cem SEO does

  • Real website audits — powered by Google PageSpeed Insights, covering performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO
  • Google Search Console integration — connect your own Search Console account to see your site's real clicks, impressions, and search ranking position, right on your dashboard
  • Keyword research — search-interest data via Google Trends, with related and rising search terms
  • Multi-site tracking — track and switch between multiple websites in one place
  • Score history — see how your site's health score changes over time, with email alerts if it drops

Who it's for

Site owners, freelancers, and small agencies who want a clear, real picture of their SEO performance without needing a desktop tool or a technical background.

Get started

Cem SEO is currently available as an Android app. Sign in with your Google account to connect Search Console and start seeing your real search performance data.
